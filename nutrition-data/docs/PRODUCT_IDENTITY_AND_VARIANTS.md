# Product Identity and Variants

Product identity is not name-only.

Identity inputs:

- manufacturer;
- brand;
- product name;
- product code;
- barcode or SKU;
- country;
- formulation region;
- package size;
- product type;
- source identifier.

Supported relationships:

- replaces;
- replaced by;
- regional variant of;
- packaging variant of;
- renamed from;
- equivalent to;
- comparable to.

Country-specific product variants must be separate records when formulation, packaging, declared analysis, price, or availability differs. A UK, Australian and US formulation under the same brand name must not be merged unless source evidence proves equivalence.
