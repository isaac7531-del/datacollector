# Safety Boundaries

The Nutrition Data Engine provides decision support and education. It does not diagnose or treat disease.

Safety warnings are generated for:

- laminitis risk;
- insulin dysregulation / EMS;
- PPID;
- tying-up history;
- kidney or liver conditions;
- pregnancy;
- lactation;
- youngstock;
- severe underweight;
- severe obesity;
- suspected deficiency;
- excessive selenium;
- excessive vitamin A or D;
- unsafe feed quantity;
- abrupt ration change;
- competition prohibited-substance warnings.

Where medical risk is present, recommendations advise consultation with a veterinarian or qualified equine nutritionist.

Clinical thresholds must be backed by named standards or integration-provided expert rules before being treated as hard limits.
